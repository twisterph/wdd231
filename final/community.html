Play Better. Respect the Court.

Good basketball culture starts with respect, communication, and knowing how to join a run.

Pickup Game Etiquette

Call next clearly, respect winners, avoid arguing every foul, and keep the game moving.

Best Time to Play

Outdoor courts are usually better early morning or evening, especially in hot cities.

Safety First

Bring water, warm up before playing, and check the court surface before sprinting or jumping.
