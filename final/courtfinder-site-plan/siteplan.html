CourtFinder Site Plan

Individual Website Project Planning Document

Site Name

CourtFinder

I selected the name CourtFinder because the website is focused on helping basketball players find and explore different basketball courts. The name is simple, easy to remember, and clearly connects to the purpose of the project.

Optional domain: courtfinderhoops.com

Site Purpose

CourtFinder will provide a modern basketball court discovery experience for players like me who want to find places to play. The site will include information about indoor and outdoor courts, court ratings, skill levels, lighting availability, best times to play, court surface, parking, and general court atmosphere.

The website will also allow users to view court details, filter courts by category, save favorite courts with local storage, and recommend a court through an HTML form.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find an outdoor basketball court with lights for evening games?
  • Which basketball courts are better for beginner players?
  • Can I save my favorite courts so I can find them again later?
  • How can I recommend a basketball court that is not listed yet?

Typography

Rajdhani

Rajdhani will be used for headings, the logo text, section titles, and important visual elements. It gives the website a sporty and modern look.

Roboto

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, forms, buttons, and general body content. It is clean, readable, and works well on both mobile and desktop screens.
